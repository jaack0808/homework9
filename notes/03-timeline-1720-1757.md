Takeaway for the letter: the ancestor's 1745–50 window contains one bad stretch for British
stocks (Dec 1745 → mid-1748) and one good stretch (late 1748 → 1750). For the VOC, every year
of the window is part of a long decline with a political crisis in 1747–48.
