## Extracts (paraphrased with page refs; short quotes)

- EIC archives show **70** confirmed or probable slaving voyages under Company auspices
  1622–1772, carrying at least **3,300** slaves, ~80% from Madagascar (p. 301).
- The Company probably traded "at least 10.000 and perhaps as many as 15.000 to 20.000
  slaves" in the 17th–18th centuries (p. 301).
- British ships carried enslaved people to Bencoolen, Madras and St Helena (p. 298).
- Dutch: the VOC, its employees and Batavian entrepreneurs imported ~50% of the 200,000–300,000
  slaves reaching Dutch Southeast Asian settlements, perhaps as many as 500,000 in total
  (17th–18th c., p. 297).

## Used for

Ethics: both companies used slavery; the VOC on a far larger scale.
