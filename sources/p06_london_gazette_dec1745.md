# *The London Gazette*, Tuesday 3 – Saturday 7 December 1745 (issue 8490)

- Type: PRIMARY (official government newspaper). Language: English.
- Fetched: https://thegazette.co.uk/London/issue/8490/data.pdf

## Extracts (verbatim; OCR)

- Stafford, 4 Dec: "We have Advice, that at Four this Morning, the Pretender's Son entered
  Derby with 450 Horse, and 5300 Foot."
- Proclamation of 6 Dec (St James's) for arresting Jesuits and priests, "a time of open
  Rebellion in Favour of a popish Pretender."
- Company notices: the Bank of England called a General Court for 12 Dec; the South Sea
  Company's transfer books to be shut; the **East India Company** gave similar notices about
  transfer books and dividends.

## Used for

Dates the London panic of "Black Friday", 6 Dec 1745 (Jacobite army at Derby, 4–6 Dec),
which Carlos & Neal (s04) say made all British securities dip sharply. The EIC's routine
transfer-book/dividend notice in the same issue shows the Company kept paying through
the crisis — consistent with the Third Report's unbroken 8% (p02).
