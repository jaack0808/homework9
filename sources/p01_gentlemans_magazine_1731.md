## Used for

- EIC ("India") stock stood at **189** per £100 in January 1731 — six years after the
  ancestor's purchase, and well above the ~149–167 range of 1724–25 (see s02). Shows the
  1725–31 rise and provides the "high" exit scenario (190).
- Illustrates that EIC was one of the three great London "moneyed company" stocks, quoted
  alongside Bank and South Sea stock.
