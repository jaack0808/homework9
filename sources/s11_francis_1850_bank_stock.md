## Extracts

From the table "Dividends, with the highest and lowest prices of Bank of England Stock"
(year · dividend · highest · lowest), as surfaced by search of the Gutenberg text:

    1748 · 5 · 129 · 117

On 1750: "The first reduction in the interest of the national debt—from four to three per
cent.—was effected in 1750"; interest reduced to 3½% for 1750–57, then 3%.

Earlier note (1690s): brokers' rumours could cause "a variation in the price of East India
Stock of 263 per cent." No EIC price table for 1720–1760 in this book (checked).

## Used for

Proxy for the shape of British "moneyed company" share prices at the end of the window:
1748 trough (117), 1749 peak (140), 1750 holding within the 1749 range (131–136). Used to
argue EIC exit late 1749–1750, not 1745–48.
