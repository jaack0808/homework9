# A. R. T. Kemasang, "The 1740 Massacre of Chinese in Java: Curtain Raiser for the Dutch Plantation Economy", *Bulletin of Concerned Asian Scholars* 14.1 (1982): 61–71

- Type: SECONDARY (scholarly article). Language: English. DOI 10.1080/14672715.1982.10412638.
- Fetched: https://www.teuru.org.nz/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/The-1740-massacre-of-Chinese-in-Java-Curtain-raiser-for-the-Dutch-plantation-economy.pdf
- Replaces the Wikipedia article used in earlier drafts.

## Extracts (p. 68–70)

- "most sources agree, 10,000 city-Chinese lost their lives" (p. 68)
- "Of the 80,000-odd Chinese in Batavia's environs prior to the extermination only around 3,000
  survived" (p. 68)
- "the Dutch distributed arms to what they themselves called 'the low-class masses'" and "gave
  these 'mobs' a free hand to massacre the helpless Chinese" (p. 68); VOC troops killed Chinese
  who fled before the curfew.
- Council of the Indies voted in June 1741 for a "general massacre of the Chinese over the whole
  of Java" (p. 69, citing Valckenier's report).
- Sugar mills "changed hands and became the property of Europeans" (p. 69).

## Note
Wikipedia's claim of a "two ducats per head" bounty is **not** in Kemasang; it was removed from
the paper because it could not be verified in a scholarly source I could read.
