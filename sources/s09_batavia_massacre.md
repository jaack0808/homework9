## Extracts

- Dates 9 Oct – 22 Nov 1740; "at least 10,000 ethnic Chinese were massacred; just 600 to
  3,000 are believed to have survived."
- Governor-General Adriaan Valckenier "declared that any uprising would be met with deadly force";
  the council offered a "reward of two ducats for every Chinese head surrendered to the soldiers."
- Valckenier convicted and condemned to death in March 1744; died in prison 1751.
- Sugar production "suffered greatly"; mills fell from 131 (1710) to 66 by 1750; production
  took until the 1760s to recover.
