# Larry Neal, "The Integration and Efficiency of the London and Amsterdam Stock Markets in the Eighteenth Century", BEBR Faculty Working Paper 1177 (Univ. of Illinois, Sept. 1985); published in *Journal of Economic History* 47 (1987)

- Type: SECONDARY. English.
- Fetched: https://www.ideals.illinois.edu/items/29066/bitstreams/97267/data.pdf

## Extracts

- EIC London/Amsterdam price correlations: 1723–1794 levels 0.993; 1739–1748 war 0.988;
  1748–1756 peace 0.997. War of the Austrian Succession "remains an anomaly."
- Average Amsterdam–London gap ~2.5 points for EIC stock.
- Dutch holdings of EIC stock rose from 13.4% (1723–24) to 21.4% (1750) (citing Dickson):
  the period when the Dutch "built up their holdings in the English joint stock companies."
- The *Amsterdamsche Courant* began quoting English shares on 9 Aug 1723 (Neal 1987 note).

## Used for

Practical point for the letter: an Amsterdam investor could buy EIC stock in Amsterdam at
essentially the London price, and many Dutch investors did so in exactly this period.
