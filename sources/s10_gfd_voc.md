## Extracts

- VOC price: participated in the bubble "when shares exceeded 1,000", "fell back to 600",
  "rallied to 800 in the 1730s, then slowly declined from there" (NB: the 800 figure conflicts
  with Gaastra's 620 in 1737 — Gaastra preferred).
- Dividends averaged around 18% of capital over the company's life; none after 1782; with
  prices mostly around 400, the yield was ~3–6%.
- Returns table: 1719–1794 price change **−2.83%/yr**, dividend 3.8%, total 0.86%/yr;
  1602–1719 total 6.93%/yr.
