## My check

Dividends of ~18.6% on f6.44m ≈ f1.2m/yr ≈ f12m per decade. In 1730–40 the European
surplus (f8.0m) was smaller than the dividends paid, and in 1740–50 roughly equal; dividends
were being paid out of borrowing — consistent with de Jong et al. (s05) and Barneveld (s12).
(Wikipedia's VOC article states the same: dividends exceeded the surplus in every decade
1690–1760 except 1710–20 — not used as a citation.)

## Used for

VOC "fortunes": the margin that supported the dividend was thin and shrinking.
