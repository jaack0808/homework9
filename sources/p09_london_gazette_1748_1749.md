# *The London Gazette*, 1748–1749 (five issues)

- Type: PRIMARY (official newspaper). Language: English. All fetched as OCR PDFs from
  thegazette.co.uk (found via search, 9 Nov 2026). Dates are Old Style: "February 1748" =
  February 1749 New Style.

## No. 8768, 2–6 August 1748 — https://thegazette.co.uk/London/issue/8768/data.pdf
- Proclamation: "Preliminaries for restoring a General Peace were signed at Aix la Chapelle on
  the Nineteenth Day" [of April last, O.S.]; Spain and Genoa acceded 17 June O.S.
- Hostilities to cease: 12 days in the Channel and North Seas; six weeks to the Line; six
  months "in all other Parts of the World, without any Exception" (i.e., India).
- Used for: dating the peace that ended the war-time slump.

## No. 8801, 26–29 November 1748 — https://www.thegazette.co.uk/London/issue/8801/page/3/data.pdf
- "East India House, Nov. 23, 1748. The Court of Directors of the United Company of Merchants of
  England trading to the East Indies" give notice of a Quarterly General Court in December; the
  transfer books to be shut in early December "at Two o'Clock, and opened again on Thursday the
  19th of January next"; "the Dividend Warrants due at Christmas next will be" ready in early
  February. (OCR garbled; rate not stated in the notice — 8% per the Third Report.)
- Used for: the EIC kept paying through and after the war; mechanics of collecting the
  Christmas dividend (books shut ~3 weeks before Christmas) → "sell after the Christmas dividend
  is secured".

## No. 8803, 3–6 December 1748 — https://thegazette.co.uk/London/issue/8803/data.pdf
- "The Directors and principal Proprietors of the India Company, of the Chamber of Amsterdam"
  resolved to "appoint the Prince of Orange Directors and Governor General of the Dutch
  Possessions in the Indies", "as far as it depends on that Chamber"; "this Affair cannot be
  entirely terminated, till a General Court of Directors and Proprietors be held".
- Used for: VOC governance crisis of 1748 (independent confirmation of De Pinto).

## No. 8806, 13–17 December 1748 — https://thegazette.co.uk/London/issue/8806/data.pdf
- Hague, Dec. 20 N.S.: "The Directors of the East-India Company made last Friday to the States
  General, their annual Report of the Situation of the Affairs of their Company."
  (Text as surfaced by the search engine's extract of the PDF.)
- Used for: the VOC board reporting to the States General while the stadtholder's takeover was
  pending.

## No. 8820, 31 January – 4 February 1748/9 — https://thegazette.co.uk/London/issue/8820/data.pdf
- Proclamation of "a Definitive Treaty of Peace and Friendship between Us, the most Christian
  King, and the States General", "concluded at Aix la Chapelle the Seventh Day of October last";
  "Given at our Court at St. James's, the First Day of February, 1748".
- Used for: the war formally over by February 1749 — start of the post-war plateau.
